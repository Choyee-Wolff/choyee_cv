The question

Which campaign patterns can help a marketing team make better targeting and planning decisions?

My approach

I used R for analysis and Tableau to communicate campaign patterns. The dashboard brings together model comparison, conversion rates, customer attributes and campaign timing.

Findings & interpretation

Look beyond accuracy

The project compares accuracy and recall to show why apparently strong predictions can still miss potential leads.

Check data quality

Missing education categories limit how confidently the relationship between education and conversion can be interpreted.

Explore campaign patterns

Views of call timing, job categories and call duration help identify questions for future campaign testing.

Scope & limitations

Observed associations do not establish that a campaign change will cause higher conversion. Call duration is only known after a call, so it needs careful treatment in any targeting model.
