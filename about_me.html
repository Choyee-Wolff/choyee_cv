About me

Curiosity, structure
and a practical focus.

I’m Choyee, a Data Analyst with an MSc in Data Science. I enjoy finding patterns in complex data and making them useful to the people making decisions.

Why I work with data

My interest in data began with a university project analysing survey responses to understand patterns in student success. I was drawn to the process of moving from a messy dataset to an explanation someone could use.

Today, my work at Stobbs spans performance reporting, business intelligence, AI search visibility and customer segmentation. I combine practical reporting with an interest in predictive modelling and explainable machine learning.

I work across the full analytical process: clarifying the question, preparing the data, choosing an appropriate method and communicating the findings. I value clear assumptions and a careful distinction between a pattern in the data and a proven business outcome.

Beyond the data

I enjoy hands-on DIY projects, learning new skills and planning travel itineraries. The same curiosity and love of organising information carry through into my analytical work.
